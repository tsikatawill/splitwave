Splitwave
Install Command line GitHub

Each app, its own speaker.

Splitwave lives in your Mac's menu bar and sends each app's sound to the output you choose. Music on your AirPods, the movie on the TV, a call on the speakers, all at once.

Download for Mac Free and open source. macOS 14.2 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

What it does

Pick an output per app

Choose a device next to any app that's playing. Add more under “Also play on” to hear one app on several outputs at once. Apps you leave alone keep using the system output.

Give every app a volume

Every app gets its own slider and mute, with its live level drawn inside the slider. macOS still has no per-app volume control; now you do.

Switch setups in one click

Save every app's outputs and volume as “Desk” or “Movie night” and switch between them from the menu, a Shortcut or a hotkey. Routes come back when a device or app reconnects.

Install

  1. Download and drag to Applications

    Open Splitwave.dmg and drag Splitwave into your Applications folder.

  2. Allow it to open

    Splitwave isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch is blocked. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Splitwave. You only do this once.

  3. Allow system audio recording

    macOS asks before any app can capture another app's sound. Splitwave needs this to move audio; it only touches the apps you route and never records or saves anything.

Command line

Everything in the menu also works from Terminal and Shortcuts. Link the tool once (it asks for your password):

sudo mkdir -p /usr/local/bin
sudo ln -sf /Applications/Splitwave.app/Contents/MacOS/splitwave /usr/local/bin/splitwave

Switch a setup from Shortcuts or a hotkey app by opening splitwave://setup/Desk.

# apps playing sound, and your outputs
splitwave list
# one app, one or more outputs
splitwave route spotify airpods
splitwave route vlc tv "bluetooth speaker"
# any app's volume
splitwave volume chrome 30
# saved setups
splitwave setup save Desk
splitwave setup apply "Movie night"

Good to know

Privacy

Splitwave runs entirely on your Mac. It has no network access, no accounts and no analytics. Audio passes straight from the app to your speakers and is never stored.

How it works

It uses the audio taps built into macOS 14.2 and later, so there's no driver or system extension to install, and uninstalling is just deleting the app.

Limits

  • Browsers play every tab through one process, so a browser routes as a whole. To split two sites, use two browsers or save one as a Safari web app (File › Add to Dock).
  • When one app plays on several outputs, Bluetooth devices run 100–200 ms behind wired ones.
  • Some copy-protected video plays silently once routed.
